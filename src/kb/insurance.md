# Declared value and insurance

Declared value is set by the sender at acceptance and costs 1% of the declared amount.
It cannot be raised after the parcel has been accepted.

Without a declared value, compensation for loss or damage is capped at 500 UAH
regardless of the actual contents. This is the single most common source of disputes.

With a declared value, we refund the actual damage up to the declared amount. Partial
damage is assessed by an appraiser; the assessment costs 250 UAH and is refunded if the
claim is upheld.

Insurance does not cover: contents that were prohibited, damage caused by inadequate
packaging by the sender, natural spoilage of perishables, and loss of value from an
opened factory seal.

A claim needs photographs of the parcel before opening, photographs of the contents and
the waybill number. Claims without photographs of the intact packaging are rejected.
