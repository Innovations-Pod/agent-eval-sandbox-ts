# Corporate accounts

A corporate account is opened on a signed contract and a company tax number. Activation
takes two business days; until then shipments go on the standard public tariff.

Corporate clients are invoiced monthly. The invoice closes on the last day of the month
and is due within 10 banking days; late payment suspends the account until settled.

Volume terms are agreed individually and recorded in an annex to the contract. Support
operators do not quote or confirm individual terms — questions about them go to the
sales department.

An account manager is assigned to accounts shipping more than 200 parcels a month.
Below that, service goes through the general support line.

API access for creating waybills is issued on request and is free. The key is bound to
the contract and is revoked when the account is suspended.
