# What we do not carry

The following are refused at acceptance and, if discovered in transit, are handed to
the authorities: weapons and ammunition, narcotics, explosives, compressed gases,
flammable liquids, poisons, radioactive materials.

Cash, bank cards, securities and jewellery above 5000 UAH declared value are not
accepted for any direction. Loss of undeclared valuables is not compensated.

Perishable food is carried on the City direction only, and only with an "urgent"
service on the same day. Frozen goods are not carried at all — we have no refrigerated
vehicles.

Medicines are accepted with a prescription copy attached to the waybill. Prescription-only
drugs are not carried on the International direction.

Batteries and devices containing them are carried at up to 30% charge, one device per
parcel, on the Domestic and City directions only.
