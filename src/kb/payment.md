# Payment

Payment is accepted in cash at drop-off points, by card online and on the courier's
terminal, and by bank transfer for corporate accounts.

Cash on delivery transfers the parcel's price from the recipient to the sender. It costs
2% of the transferred amount with a minimum of 20 UAH, and the money reaches the sender
within 3 banking days of delivery.

Cash on delivery is not available on the International direction and is capped at
50,000 UAH per parcel.

Who pays the delivery charge is chosen at acceptance: the sender, the recipient, or a
third-party corporate account. Changing the payer after acceptance is not possible.

A receipt is issued at payment and duplicated by email. A VAT invoice for a corporate
account is generated with the monthly closing statement, not per shipment.
