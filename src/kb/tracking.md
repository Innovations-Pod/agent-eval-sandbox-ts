# Tracking statuses

| Status | What it means | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted | The parcel is in our warehouse | up to 4 hours |
| In transit | Moving between terminals | 1–2 days |
| Customs | Undergoing clearance | 1–3 days |
| Out for delivery | With the courier | same day |
| Held | Awaiting documents or payment | up to 5 days |
| Delivered | Handed over and signed for | — |

A tracking number looks like TRK-0000: three letters, a hyphen and four digits. Numbers
are issued at acceptance and never reused.

Status is refreshed when a parcel is scanned at a terminal, not continuously. A gap of
up to 24 hours between scans on the Domestic direction is normal and is not a delay.

A "Held" status older than 5 days turns into a return to sender. The sender is notified
by SMS twice: on the first day and on the fourth.
