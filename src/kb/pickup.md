# Pickup, drop-off and storage

Courier pickup is ordered until 15:00 for the same day, and any time for the next.
Pickup costs 40 UAH in the City direction and 60 UAH elsewhere; it is free for
shipments above 1000 UAH.

Drop-off points accept parcels from 09:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 16:00
on Saturday. Sunday is closed except for the terminals at airports.

A parcel waits at the destination point for 7 calendar days free of charge. From the
eighth day storage costs 20 UAH a day, and after 14 days the parcel returns to the
sender at the sender's expense.

The recipient can redirect a parcel to another point once, free of charge, while the
status is still "In transit". Redirecting after "Out for delivery" costs a new
delivery charge.

Delivery to the door is available in cities with a population above 100,000. Elsewhere
delivery is to the drop-off point only.
