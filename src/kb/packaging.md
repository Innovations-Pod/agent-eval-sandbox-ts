# Packaging requirements

| Box class | Outer size (cm) | Max weight | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| XS | 24 × 17 × 10 | 2 kg | 15 UAH |
| S | 33 × 23 × 15 | 5 kg | 22 UAH |
| M | 40 × 30 × 20 | 15 kg | 35 UAH |
| L | 60 × 40 × 30 | 30 kg | 60 UAH |

A shipment must fit its box without deforming the walls. If the contents press against
the sides, the next class up is used and the difference is charged to the sender.

Fragile goods travel in a box one class larger than the contents require, with at least
3 cm of filler on every side. The "Fragile" label costs 25 UAH and moves the parcel to
the top layer during loading — it does not increase the compensation limit.

Liquids are accepted only in factory packaging with an intact seal, inside a sealed bag.
Any leak makes the sender liable for cleaning the vehicle: 900 UAH flat.

Own packaging is allowed if it is rigid, dry and carries no old labels or barcodes.
Bags, sacks and film-wrapped bundles are not accepted for the International direction.
