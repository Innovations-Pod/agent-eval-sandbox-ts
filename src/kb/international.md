# International shipments and customs

Every EU shipment needs an invoice in two copies, a completed CN23 declaration and the
sender's tax number. Without the tax number the parcel is held at the terminal and the
sender is notified.

Goods worth up to 150 EUR enter the EU without customs duty, but VAT of the destination
country applies from the first euro. Duty above that threshold is paid by the recipient
on delivery.

Customs clearance takes 1–3 business days and is not counted in the delivery window.
This is the most common reason a shipment looks stuck in tracking while nothing is wrong.

We do not deliver to the Russian Federation, Belarus, Iran or North Korea. Shipments to
these destinations are refused at acceptance and the delivery charge is refunded in full.

Returns from the EU are paid by the sender and cost the outbound charge plus 20%. A
customs-refused parcel comes back within 21 days.
